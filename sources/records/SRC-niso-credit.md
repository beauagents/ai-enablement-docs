## Summary

CRediT is a community-owned taxonomy of 14 contributor roles designed to represent key types of contributions typically made to research outputs, including journal articles. It was originally developed to complement traditional authorship by capturing diverse contributions behind published research. Contributorship information can support research assessment, expert discovery, research integrity, and accountability. CRediT is licensed for permissive reuse under CC-BY 4.0, widely adopted, and available in multiple languages.
