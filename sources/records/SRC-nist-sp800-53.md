## Summary

This publication provides a catalog of security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations to protect organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation from diverse threats and risks. The controls are flexible and customizable and are implemented as part of an organization-wide process to manage risk. The controls address requirements derived from mission and business needs, laws, executive orders, directives, regulations, policies, standards, and guidelines.

## Parsed evidence

- The publication includes 20 control families, including Access Control; Audit and Accountability; Incident Response; Privacy; and Supply Chain Risk Management. ^ev-1
- The controls address hostile attacks, human errors, natural disasters, structural failures, foreign intelligence entities, and privacy risks. ^ev-2
- The consolidated control catalog addresses security and privacy from both functionality and assurance perspectives. ^ev-3
- Supplemental materials include an analysis of updates between SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and Rev. 4. ^ev-4
- Rev. 5 controls are available in JSON, XML, and YAML using the Open Security Controls Assessment Language (OSCAL). ^ev-5
- A minor release, SP 800-53 Release 5.2.0, was issued on August 27, 2025, with new controls and control enhancements, revisions to existing controls, and updates to control discussions and related controls. ^ev-6
