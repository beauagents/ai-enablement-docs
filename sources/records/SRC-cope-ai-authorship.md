## Summary

COPE states that AI tools such as ChatGPT and Large Language Models cannot be listed as authors of a paper. AI tools cannot meet authorship requirements because they cannot take responsibility for submitted work, assert the presence or absence of conflicts of interest, or manage copyright and license agreements. Authors using AI tools in manuscript writing, production of images or graphical elements, or collection and analysis of data must disclose how and which tool was used in the Materials and Methods or similar section. Authors remain fully responsible for all manuscript content, including content produced by an AI tool, and are liable for breaches of publication ethics.
