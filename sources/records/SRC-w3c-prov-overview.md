## Summary

This document provides a non-normative overview of the PROV Family of Documents and a roadmap to using them. PROV defines a model, corresponding serializations, and supporting definitions for interoperable interchange of provenance information in heterogeneous environments such as the Web. Provenance is information about entities, activities, and people involved in producing a piece of data or thing, which can be used to assess quality, reliability, or trustworthiness. PROV consists of 12 documents covering users, developers, and advanced audiences.

## Parsed evidence

- PROV-DM is the conceptual data model defining a common vocabulary for describing provenance. ^ev-1
- PROV supports representing and interchanging provenance using formats such as RDF and XML. ^ev-2
- PROV provides definitions for accessing provenance information, validating it, and mapping it to Dublin Core. ^ev-3
- The PROV Family of Documents includes PROV-PRIMER, PROV-O, PROV-DM, PROV-N, PROV-CONSTRAINTS, PROV-XML, PROV-AQ, PROV-DICTIONARY, PROV-DC, PROV-SEM, and PROV-LINKS, in addition to PROV-OVERVIEW. ^ev-4
- All terms defined within PROV use the namespace http://www.w3.org/ns/prov# and the prefix convention prov. ^ev-5
- The Provenance Working Group encourages implementation of the specifications overviewed in this document. ^ev-6
