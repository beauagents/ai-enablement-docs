## Summary

The paper discusses accidents in machine learning systems, defined as unintended and harmful behavior that may emerge from poor design of real-world AI systems. It presents five practical research problems related to accident risk: avoiding side effects, avoiding reward hacking, scalable supervision, safe exploration, and distributional shift. The problems are categorized according to whether they originate from the wrong objective function, an objective function that is too expensive to evaluate frequently, or undesirable behavior during learning. The paper reviews previous work, suggests research directions relevant to cutting-edge AI systems, and considers how to think productively about the safety of forward-looking AI applications.
