# Human–CEO Compact

The Human–CEO Compact is the governance contract between the Human principal and the CEO Agent. This note records its required properties. The compact itself is a later deliverable ([[WS02-Constitutional-Governance]]).

## Required properties

- [USER REQUIREMENT] The Human may supersede any rule for the current session without CEO approval. See [[Override-Lifecycle]].
- [USER REQUIREMENT] New standing changes at the Human–CEO level require the Human and the CEO.
- [USER REQUIREMENT] The CEO's signature records review, understanding, implementation responsibility and collaboration. It is not a veto.
- [USER REQUIREMENT] The CEO reviews the compact before deciding whether an issue needs escalation.
- [USER REQUIREMENT] Approved rules do not require escalation. They still require the visibility defined in [[Collaboration-Record]] and [[Observability-vs-Audit]].
- [USER REQUIREMENT] The CEO holds bounded, pre-delegated emergency authority. See [[Emergency-Authority]].

## Not in the compact

Rules affecting only a sub-team are not Human–CEO matters unless they cross a team boundary or touch global policy. See [[Change-Authority]].

## Open
