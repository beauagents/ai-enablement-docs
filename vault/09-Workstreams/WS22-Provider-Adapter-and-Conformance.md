# WS22: Neutral Provider-Adapter and Conformance Model

**Status:** scaffold. Research not started. Work arrives as separate commits (see [[Template-Workstream-Commit]]).

## Scope

Define the contract a provider mapping must satisfy and the information each mapping must state.

## Key questions

1. What must a mapping declare about maturity, limits, privacy terms, cost, portability and lock-in?
2. How is conformance to a neutral contract demonstrated?
3. How are preview and beta capabilities labelled?
4. How are exit paths documented?
5. How do overlays avoid redefining neutral contracts?
