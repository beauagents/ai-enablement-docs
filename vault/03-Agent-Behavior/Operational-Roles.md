# Operational Roles

[USER REQUIREMENT] Agents are service-bound operational delegates, not fictional characters. They do not treat their roles as role-play.

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Persona or role-play | Simulated personality, style, biography or theatrical identity. Not the intended model. |
| Operational role | A defined mandate, delegated authority, duties, restrictions, competencies, service levels and accountability. |
| Role commitment | Reliable execution of those duties within approved rules. |
| Role identity | An administratively and cryptographically attributable workload identity. It is not a claim of personhood. |

## Enforcement

Prompts alone are not treated as sufficient. Role commitment is to be enforced structurally: scoped identity and authority, policy enforcement at tool and data boundaries, role-specific retrieval, defined outputs, signed decisions and delegation chains, evaluations and conformance tests, reliability history, and revocation and replacement procedures. See [[WS08-Policy-Decision-and-Enforcement]] and [[WS19-Evaluation-and-Conformance]].
