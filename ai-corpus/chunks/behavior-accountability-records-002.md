Parent: [[Accountability-Records#Learning loop]]

Preserve evidence, contain active impact, correct recoverable effects, identify contributing conditions without inventing intent, update a test, control or proposed rule, validate against the failure case and regressions, submit permanent changes through the applicable authority, and retain the prior decision with its supersession history.

A mistake is either avoided or becomes a learning that has been turned into a tested change. Blame and ceremony are not the purpose.
