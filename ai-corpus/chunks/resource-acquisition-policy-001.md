Parent: [[Resource-Acquisition-Policy#Policy elements (neutral)]]

- Inventory of subscriptions, credits and promotional allowances, with expiry dates.
- Evaluation of cost, capability, privacy terms, reliability and exit risk.
- Provider-substitution rules: what must stay portable.
- Approval thresholds by cost and risk.
- Migration triggers, for example when a credit ends or terms change.
- A prohibition on lowering cost by obscuring risk from the Human or the governance chain.
- Records of the decision and the reasons ([[Decisions-Register]]).
