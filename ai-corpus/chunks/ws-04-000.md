## Scope

Curate mechanisms that reinforce dependable service to the Human while resisting gaming.

## Key questions

1. Which published reward and incentive designs address specification gaming and reward hacking?
2. How are sycophancy and verbosity gaming detected?
3. How is the evaluation system protected from agents?
4. How should team-level and individual incentives interact?
5. How is delegated authority reduced and restored after failure and correction?

## Option families to compare

[RECOMMENDATION: compare all credible options. Do not select.]

- Outcome rewards
- Process and compliance rewards
- Human evaluation
- Independent verifier scoring
- Delayed outcome evaluation
- Reputation and reliability history
- Authority-based incentives (scope grows or shrinks)
