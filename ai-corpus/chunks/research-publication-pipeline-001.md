Parent: [[Publication-Pipeline#Stages]]

1. Detect potentially novel findings, methods, taxonomies, evaluations, datasets or negative results.
2. Preserve drafts, evidence, provenance, methods, limitations and contribution records.
3. Search prior art and related literature before claiming novelty.
4. Check confidentiality, privacy, licensing, contractual, security and intellectual-property restrictions.
5. Separate validated results from hypotheses and promotional claims.
6. Reproduce or independently validate important findings.
7. Propose routes: repository paper, technical report, preprint, dataset, benchmark, conference or journal paper, public documentation.
8. Prepare branding and later monetization options without changing findings.
9. Require Human authorization before any external publication.
