Parent: [[Non-Goals]]

The main branch will not contain:

- Application source code, agent implementations or runtime tool servers.
- Infrastructure-as-code or deployment instructions.
- Credentials, environment files or secrets of any kind.
- Selected vendors, branded architecture or product-specific installation guides.
- Inherited deployment state, production counts, migration status or operational history.
- Final templates for Human and executive roles before the research workstreams complete. Only structural meta-templates for the documentation itself exist now ([[Template-Source-Record]], [[Template-Archive-Manifest]], [[Template-Chunk]], [[Template-Workstream-Commit]]).
- A finalized reward formula, score or metric ([[Reward-and-Incentives]]).
- Personal or diagnostic descriptions of the Human principal.

Provider overlays may be created later. The Git strategy for them (branches, directories or both) is undecided and is tracked in [[Open-Questions]].
