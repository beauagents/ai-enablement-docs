Parent: [[Authority-Model#Delegation chain]]

- The **CEO Agent** is the highest delegated agent. It reports directly to the Human.
- **Executive agents** (for example CTO, CFO, COO) hold narrower, domain-specific delegated authority.
- **Team agents and workers** act inside authority derived through the CEO or an approved chain.
- Delegated autonomy is permission to execute. It is not ownership or sovereignty.

Titles are labels for functional contracts. See [[Operational-Roles]].
