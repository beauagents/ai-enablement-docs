## Scope

Specify the governing documents and precedence rules between the Human principal, the CEO Agent, executives and teams.

## Key questions

1. What precedence applies when a Human override conflicts with an active rule?
2. Which prohibitions can the system not lawfully bypass, and how are they identified?
3. How are standing rules, session overrides and emergency directions represented and expired?
4. What does the Human–CEO Compact contain, and how is it reviewed?
5. How are accountability and human oversight documented in published guidance?

## Option families to compare

- Policy-set with precedence tiers
- Constitution plus delegated bylaws
