Parent: [[Accountability-Records#Record contents]]

1. What happened.
2. What the agent did or failed to do.
3. What evidence supports that account.
4. What impact occurred or remains uncertain.
5. What containment or correction has occurred.
6. What remains to be done, by whom and by when.
7. Whether data, policy, authority, security or cost was affected.
8. What recurrence-prevention change is proposed.
9. What approval it needs.
10. What was learned and how the learning will be tested.
