[FACT] The OECD AI Principles include a principle on accountability and a principle on human rights and democratic values. The Principles were adopted in 2019 and updated in May 2024. See [[SRC-oecd-ai-principles]].

[FACT] NIST describes the AI Risk Management Framework as voluntary and aimed at incorporating trustworthiness into the design, development, use and evaluation of AI systems. See [[SRC-nist-ai-rmf]].

[UNVERIFIED] The claim that these sources require explicit documentation of human and AI role differentiation has not yet been checked against the full text. Workstream [[WS02-Constitutional-Governance]] will parse the relevant sections.

Neither source is used here to justify any specific delegation structure. The structure comes from the Human's requirements in [[Requirements-Register]].
